# On the Horizon — "Undiscovered · Be the First" email series

Six emails over five weeks. Every email links to its first-look page at `/horizon/[slug]`, where readers join the "Be the first" list. Sign-ups are saved to Airtable › Inquiries with Occasion = "Be the first · On the Horizon".
James approved his opening lines on 30 Oct 2026.

**Before sending**
- The HTML launch email is `emails/be-the-first-launch.html`. Paste it into Klaviyo as an HTML template. It uses `{{ organization.url }}`, so set the site URL in Klaviyo › Settings › Organization first.
- **Who receives it:** past inquiries, VIP list and journal subscribers. Leave out anyone who has booked in the last 30 days.
- **Tracking:** every link carries `utm_campaign=on-the-horizon-01` and the place's slug as `utm_content`, so Google Analytics shows which place pulled interest.
- **Ticking it off:** in Airtable › Horizon, tick **Email sent** on the row once its email has gone.

## More "Be the first" ideas
1. **Founding families:** the first three families to book a new place get a handwritten Ledger card and a call with James before they travel.
2. **The Horizon postcard:** a quarterly printed card in the On the Horizon stamp style, mailed to VIP families. It prints straight from the kit files.
3. **Show "Opening soon" dates in search:** once a place publishes firm dates, add Event or Offer markup to its page so the dates can appear in Google results.
4. **"Who's been?" reply prompt:** ask readers who have stayed at a Horizon place to reply. Their notes become questions on James's vetting checklist, never fake reviews.
5. **Instagram Story series:** one place a day for five days, each with the stamp, the one-line family reason and a "Be the first" link sticker.
6. **Moving into the Ledger:** when James vets a Horizon place and it becomes a Ledger house, send a short "It earned its stamp" email. That shows readers the watch list means something.
